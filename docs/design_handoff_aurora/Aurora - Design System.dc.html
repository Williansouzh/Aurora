Aurora Life OS
The Quiet design system
A calm, private space where someone watches themselves become who they want to be. The interface stays out of the way: warm paper neutrals, editorial serif headlines, a single indigo for action, and gamification that whispers rather than shouts.
01 — Foundations
Principles
Calm over loud
Generous whitespace, muted color, no gradients or noise. The user's life is the content.
Type leads
A serif headline gives every screen a human, journal-like voice. Numbers are set in serif too.
Reward, quietly
Streaks, levels and XP are present but understated — a small flame, a thin progress bar.
